## PRODUCT OVERVIEW

### What We're Building

An AI-powered trading signals platform that:
1. **Analyzes** real-time market data (price, volume, news, sentiment)
2. **Predicts** market movements using ML models
3. **Generates** BUY/SELL/HOLD signals with confidence scores
4. **Alerts** users via push, email, SMS, Discord
5. **Tracks** performance (win rate, ROI per signal)
6. **Explains** why each signal was generated

**Signal Generation**
- Real-time analysis every minute
- Multiple asset classes: Crypto, Stocks, Forex, Commodities
- Confidence scoring: 0-100%

**User Experience**

- Web dashboard with interactive charts
- Push notifications for alerts
- Discord/Telegram integration
- Email summaries

**Performance Tracking**
- Win rate per signal
- ROI tracking

- Compare your results vs benchmark

**Advanced Features** (Elite tier)
- Custom alert rules
- Backtesting tool (test strategies on historical data)
- API access for automated trading bots
- Advanced technical analysis
- Correlation analysis

## RISK ANALYSIS & MITIGATION

### Key Risks

**Risk 1: Signal Accuracy Doesn't Improve**
- Impact: High (affects retention)
- Probability: Medium
- Mitigation:
  - Hire experienced ML engineer early
  - Build ensemble of models
  - Continuous A/B testing
  - Partner with academic researchers

**Risk 2: High Churn Rate (70%+ lose money)**
- Impact: High (affects LTV)
- Probability: High
- Mitigation:
  - Track actual user ROI vs signals
  - Implement stop-loss recommendations
  - Transparent win rate dashboard
  - Money-back guarantee for first month
  - Educational content about risk

**Risk 3: Regulatory Issues**
- Impact: Critical
- Probability: Medium
- Mitigation:
  - Consult with securities lawyer
  - Disclaimer: "Not investment advice"
  - No managed accounts (user responsibility)
  - Compliance officer on advisory board

**Risk 4: Market Crash Reduces Trading Volume**
- Impact: High (less trading = less usage)
- Probability: Medium
- Mitigation:
  - Diversify across multiple markets
  - Hedging strategies built in
  - Counter-trend signals
  - Recession-proof with bonds/treasuries

**Risk 5: Competition from Well-Funded Startups**
- Impact: High
- Probability: High
- Mitigation:
  - Move fast (get 50K users before Series A)
  - Build moat through data + accuracy
  - Network effects (user community)
  - Superior UX

**Risk 6: Dependency on External APIs**
- Impact: Medium
- Probability: Low
- Mitigation:
  - Multiple data source fallbacks
  - Direct market data partnerships
  - Build internal data pipeline
